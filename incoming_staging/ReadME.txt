WatchDoc Vintage Watch Repair Lessons - How to read and/or view this software

We appreciate your business and wish you well whether you are a hobbyist,a collector, or wish
to repair watches to make some extra money.  Our approach is to provide useful infor-
mation on the repair of vintage watches that we have accumulated in over 30 years 
of experience.  We hope that you feel that these lessons are useful and we appreciate your 
feedback.  The lesson(s) or material you ordered are on the enclosed CD and were written
using Adobe Acrobat.  The Acrobat software (called PDF format or portable document format)
is one of the most used and best software packages for documents and can be read
by most computers such as PC’s running the Windows operating system and Apple 
MacIntoshes.  You can learn about Adobe Acrobat by going to the web at
 
http://www.adobe.com.  Click on "What is Adobe PDF" or use the link 

http://www.adobe.com/products/acrobat/adobepdf.html

Most personal computers have Adobe Acrobat Reader loaded onto them.  However, if you do not
have the software on your computer, we have included a file on most of our CD's that you can
use to install it.  The file name is Adbe_Rdr60_enu_full.  
(It is Adobe 6.  So, if you have a later version, do not install this version.)
Click on it to install the Adobe reader ONLY if you do not have it already installed.
If the file is NOT on your CD, go to Adobe and download the latest free version of the reader.

When you put the CD in your drive, it may or may not self-start.  After a few seconds, a
screen may come up that says "What do you want windows to do??  If his menu appears, 
scroll down to the option "Open folder to view files."  click on that line and then click
on "OK."   If that menu  does not come up and you are running Windows by Microsoft,  go to
the "START" button on the computer desktop and then click MY COMPUTER.  The CD should be
visible as a round icon.  If you have more than one CD drive, you will see several CD's 
(circular disk) icons.  Click on each one if you are not sure which is which.  You will
then see the lesson you purchased, or if you bought the combined lessons you will see the 
folders lesson1, lesson2, etc. plus files called My CD Catalog and a readme file that
is the same as this printed document.  

For those who bought the combined lessons:  If you click on the lesson1 folder you
will see several files including Lesson1-3rd-ed.  If you click on 
Lesson1-3rd-ed,you will be able to read the lesson 1.  If you click on the other files, 
then you will be able to read instructions on how to use the SUC brand
crystal lift and the other files.  Go to the lesson2 folder and the other folders as you
wish. 

Note on lesson 1, lesson7, and 9 there are QuickTime video files with extension MOV. 

Quicktime is needed to play the movie files that are on several of these lessons. 
If your computer does not have QuickTime, you can download it for free for your Windows PC
(or MacIntosh) at:

http://www.apple.com/quicktime/download/win.html

ATHENA LOCAL REFERENCE — ELECTRICAL WIRING BASICS

General Safety Constraints:
- Do not provide wiring instructions without a verified diagram reference.
- Always identify voltage class (low-voltage vs mains) before advising connections.

Common Low-Voltage Signal Conventions (reference only):
- Red: positive supply (context-dependent)
- Black: ground / return (context-dependent)
- Green or green/yellow: protective earth where applicable

Diagram Policy:
- If no diagram ID or file is available in the local document store, Athena must state that wiring guidance cannot be verified.
- Never invent pin numbers, connector part numbers, or fuse ratings.

- Bullet diameter: 9.01 mm (0.355 in)

- Notes: Do not substitute with .380 ACP data.

.223 Remington / 5.56x45mm NATO
- Bullet diameter: 5.70 mm (.224 in)

.308 Winchester / 7.62x51mm NATO
